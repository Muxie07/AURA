# AURA CLIENT DEMO PRESENTATION SCRIPT

**Total Time:** 5 Minutes
**Target Audience:** Non-technical business users, VCs, Enterprise Clients

## 1. The Command Center (Dashboard)
**Action:** Go to `http://localhost:3000`
**Talking Point:** "Welcome to AURA. This is the Command Center where we monitor the AI's operations across the enterprise. Unlike chatbots, AURA is an autonomous system. Right away, you see how many 'AI Tasks' it has executed, its active workload, and any actions that require your manual human approval."

## 2. Company Knowledge 
**Action:** Click **Company Knowledge** in the sidebar.
**Talking Point:** "AURA isn't just a generic AI; it knows *your* business. Here we safely upload our internal policies, runbooks, and documents. The AI securely searches this specific knowledge base whenever it handles tasks, guaranteeing it uses our actual procedures."

## 3. Available Actions (Tools)
**Action:** Click **Available Actions** in the sidebar.
**Talking Point:** "AURA can take action. Here is the registry of everything AURA is authorized to do—from searching CRM data to restarting servers. Notice the 'Security' column. Routine read-only tasks auto-execute, but high-risk operations are strictly cordoned off behind Human Approval."

## 4. Ask AURA (Conversations)
**Action:** Click **Conversations** in the sidebar.
**Action:** Click the example prompt: *"Investigate our recent API latency."* and hit Send.
**Talking Point:** "Let's put AURA to work. We ask a simple business question. Watch as AURA seamlessly takes our prompt, queries the Company Knowledge, decides what tools to use, and streams a structured response back to us in real-time."

## 5. Action Required (HITL Approvals)
**Action:** Click **Approvals** in the sidebar.
**Talking Point:** "If AURA decides it needs to modify a production system or send an email, it stops and waits for us here. The interface tells us in plain English: *'AURA wants to: Restart Service'* and explains *Why*. We retain full control to securely Approve or Reject."

## 6. Observability (Audit Log)
**Action:** Click **Observability** in the sidebar.
**Talking Point:** "For complete transparency, every single decision AURA makes is logged. Here we can see the exact timeline—from when the request was received, to the AI analyzing it, to the specific tools it executed. No black boxes."
